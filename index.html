From language to understanding.

I am learning about artificial intelligence and exploring simulated cognition in language models. Drawing on philosophy of language, linguistics, and Lacanian psychoanalysis, I use my proposed cognitive chain as a reference for quantification and model prototypes.

The long-term aims are reliable understanding, less hallucination and opacity, deeper understanding of art, culture, and politics, and innovative problem-solving. Consciousness remains an open research question.
